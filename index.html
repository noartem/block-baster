How to play

Drag the pieces onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Clear several lines at once, or keep clearing on consecutive turns, for combo bonuses. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

Pieces glowing in the tray are a lucky set, picked to clear lines and free up room. At first every set is lucky, then every 2nd from 2k points, every 3rd from 10k and every 4th from 50k. When the board is half full, the next set is always lucky.

Clear the whole board to switch to a new look. Score 10k, 50k, 100k, 200k and 500k points in one game to unlock fancier styles, with new blocks, effects, fonts and sounds.
