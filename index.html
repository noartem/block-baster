How to play

Drag the pieces onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Clear several lines at once, or keep clearing on consecutive turns, for combo bonuses. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

Clear the whole board to switch to a new look. Score 50k, 100k, 200k, 500k and 1M points in one game to unlock fancier styles, with new blocks, effects, fonts and sounds.
